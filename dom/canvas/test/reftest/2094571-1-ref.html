Every canvas below draws light blue content, rgb(64, 64, 255), with ctx.filter = "hue-rotate(240deg)", which turns it green, rgb(0, 131, 0). Every square should be green. An orange-red square, rgb(255, 101, 65), means red and blue were swapped while the filter was applied. Hover over a square to see its pixel value.

The 512×512 canvases are accelerated when accelerated canvas is enabled (about:support, Canvas 2D). The 16×16 canvases are below gfx.canvas.accelerated.min-size and stay in software.
